## Full description

FLOOR is a cooperative investment house — Riot energy, Reddit rooms, Polymarket-style books, and a members’ bazaar — built as a demonstration.

The tape is public diligence. Sort Hot, New, Top, or Following. Restack a thread. Nested comments on a rent roll.

Vaults are the funds. Ground can own Street-to-Suite and the other commons books. The climb is apartments, a McDonald’s ground lease, desert dirt, a tour fleet. Penthouse is the illiquid book — rally cars and private deals.

Markets are labeled. Take a side on occupancy, a Fed cut, or same-store sales. Paper contracts. House credits. Not real-money gambling.

The Commons grows with membership: lounge hours, crash pads, donated nights, a house car when the count clears.

This app does not take bank deposits, does not execute real trades, and does not offer securities. Balances you see are demonstration buying power. Eighteen and up.
